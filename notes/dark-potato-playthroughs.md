# THE DARK POTATO — Two Worked Playthroughs

These are complete games played out as written in the Draft 1 handbook, using invented players. The point is to see how the game actually *feels* in motion, and where it snags.

---

# GAME ONE — 5 players, Standard Mode

**Setup:** Jerry 1 / Pear 1 / Dark Potato 1 / Ramen Bowls 2

| Player | Role |
|---|---|
| Alex | 🐭 Jerry *(public)* |
| Bea | 🍜 Ramen Bowl |
| Cam | 🍐 The Pear |
| Dee | 🥔 The Dark Potato |
| Eli | 🍜 Ramen Bowl |

Everyone knows Alex is Jerry. Nobody knows anything else.

---

## ROUND 1

### Step 1 — Ingredients

- **Alex (Jerry)** sees the topic: **instant noodles**
- **Bea** sees: *dry, hot*
- **Cam** sees: *minutes, packet*
- **Eli** sees: *salty, cheap*
- **Dee (Dark Potato)** sees the decoy: **mouldy cheese**

Dee has no idea what the real topic is. All Dee knows is that it isn't mouldy cheese, and that whatever they type has to sound like it belongs.

### Step 2 — Clues

> **Bea:** you have to wait for it to soften up properly or it's not nice
> **Cam:** honestly it's ready way faster than you'd expect
> **Eli:** it costs almost nothing which is the main appeal
> **Dee:** the smell is stronger than people expect tbh

Alex reads silently.

**Alex's read:** Bea, Cam and Eli all describe something quick, cheap and prepared. Dee talks about smell — which fits noodles poorly. Dee is the obvious suspect.

**Dee's read:** Dee learns nothing useful. Bea, Cam and Eli all sound the same, because they are the same. Nothing marks Cam as The Pear.

*Whispers this round:*
- Bea → Eli: "dee's clue was weird right"
- Eli → Bea: "yeah smell? of what"
- Dee → Cam: "i think bea's fishing, watch her"

### Step 3 — Voting

Dee uses **Murder**, and has to choose between Bea, Cam and Eli with no information at all. Dee picks Bea — because Bea is the one whispering most, which reads as engaged rather than guilty, but it's a guess.

Alex holds Ahh Shoot. Alex is fairly confident about Dee, but a miss kills Alex, so Alex waits one more round.

**Community vote:** Bea → Dee, Eli → Dee, Cam → Skip, Dee → Cam, Alex → Dee.

Three votes on Dee out of five living players. **Majority reached — Dee is eliminated.**

Murder resolves after the vote: Bea dies too.

### Result

Every Dark Potato is dead. **Jerry, The Pear and the Ramen Bowls win.** Roles are revealed.

---

## What Game One shows

**The game ended in a single round, and the clue phase worked exactly as intended.** Dee's bluff was thin, four players read it, and the vote landed. That part of the design is sound and genuinely fun — it's a clean Insider/Spyfall-style pressure round.

**But look at what The Pear actually did:** nothing. Cam posted one clue and voted skip. Cam's role had no bearing on any decision Cam made, and no bearing on anything anyone else did either. The Pear was a spectator with a win condition attached.

**And Dee's Murder was a blind 1-in-3.** If Dee had picked Cam instead of Bea, the game would have ended in an instant Dark Potato win — *before the vote was even counted, and despite Dee's bluff having completely failed.* Four players correctly identified the imposter and would still have lost.

That's the core tension in the current design: the clue-and-vote game is good, but the win conditions sit on top of it almost independently, and can resolve by luck before skill has any chance to matter.

# GAME TWO — 7 players, Sylly Mode

**Setup:** Jerry 1 / Pear 1 / Dark Potato 1 / Ramen Bowls 3 / Diarrhea 1

| Player | Role |
|---|---|
| Alex | 🐭 Jerry *(public)* |
| Bea | 🍜 Ramen Bowl |
| Cam | 🍜 Ramen Bowl |
| Dee | 🥔 The Dark Potato |
| Eli | 🍐 The Pear |
| Fin | 💩 Diarrhea |
| Gus | 🍜 Ramen Bowl |

---

## ROUND 1

### Step 1 — Ingredients

- **Alex (Jerry)** sees: **thunderstorm**
- **Bea:** *loud, sudden*
- **Cam:** *dark, sky*
- **Eli:** *wet, warning*
- **Fin:** *flash, count*
- **Gus:** *shelter, wind*
- **Dee (Dark Potato)** sees the decoy: **a broken vending machine**

Dee's food bank word for **Face Reveal** this round is **"crumbs."** If Dee can work "crumbs" naturally into a clue, Face Reveal unlocks.

### Step 2 — Clues

Dee opens with **Eat Ramen**, silencing Gus for the round. Gus's screen greys out — he can't post or whisper.

> **Bea:** it makes you jump even when you knew it was coming
> **Cam:** everything goes a weird colour beforehand
> **Eli:** you get a bit of notice if you're paying attention
> **Dee:** it always leaves a mess behind, crumbs everywhere, annoying
> **Fin:** i always end up counting under my breath

Gus is silent. Everyone can see he's been silenced, and everyone knows that means a Dark Potato is active.

**Face Reveal unlocks** — Dee slipped "crumbs" in, though it landed badly. Dee checks **Eli**, and sees: 🍐 **The Pear.**

Dee now knows exactly who to kill, in round one, before a single vote.

*Whispers:*
- Bea → Cam: "crumbs?? crumbs of WHAT"
- Cam → Bea: "yeah dee's cooked"
- Alex → Fin: "keep an eye on dee"
- Dee → Alex: *(via Impersonating, appearing as Cam)* "alex i think fin's acting weird"

### Step 3 — Voting

Dee uses **Murder** on **Eli.**

**The Pear Has Been Moldified.** Every screen fills.

Fin's **Get Pooped On** and the community vote never resolve — the game is already over.

### Result

**The Dark Potato wins,** in round one, with the entire table correctly suspecting them.

---

## What Game Two shows

**Face Reveal plus Murder is a two-card combo that wins the game outright on round one**, with no bluffing required and no way for anyone to intervene. Face Reveal costs only a slipped word — and even though Dee's use of "crumbs" was clumsy and immediately drew suspicion, it didn't matter, because suspicion has no time to become a vote. The whole social layer of the game — clues, whispers, reads, voting — was bypassed entirely.

**Diarrhea never got to exist.** Fin had one ability, usable in the voting phase, and the game ended during the voting phase before it resolved. In any game where The Dark Potato finds The Pear early, Diarrhea is a Ramen Bowl with extra steps.

**Eat Ramen leaks information.** Silencing Gus told everyone a Dark Potato was in play and active — but since Dee won immediately, the cost never came due.

---

# OVERALL READ

**The strong core:** the clue round is legitimately good. Jerry holding the topic, everyone else describing around it, and one player bluffing blind — that's a proven, satisfying loop, and Game One's first four messages show it working exactly as intended.

**The problem:** three separate mechanics can end the game instantly and independently of that loop.

1. **Murder** — an unconditional kill, every round, that wins if it lands on The Pear.
2. **Face Reveal** — cheap, early, and turns the coin flip into a certainty.
3. **The Pear having no tell** — which means before Face Reveal, it's pure luck, and after Face Reveal, it's a formality.

Put together: a Dark Potato who plays badly still wins about a third of the time at low counts, and a Dark Potato who plays the combo wins nearly always. Meanwhile the good team's clue-reading — the actual game — only matters in the narrow window where neither of those has fired.

**Worth considering:** how much of the ability list would be missed if The Dark Potato's win required convincing the *table* to vote out The Pear, rather than being able to kill them privately? Both games above would have kept going, and both would have been decided by the part of the game that's working.

**Also worth noting:** the Pear currently has no gameplay of their own in either simulation. Whatever fix lands for the "no tell" problem is also the fix for the Pear being a passenger — giving The Pear something they have to actively conceal is what would turn the role into a part to play rather than a label to hold.
