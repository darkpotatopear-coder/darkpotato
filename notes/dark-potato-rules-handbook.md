# THE DARK POTATO

## 1. THE IDEA

One player is **Jerry**, and everybody knows it. Jerry secretly holds a topic — say, *instant noodles*. Everyone except Jerry and The Dark Potato is given two words related to that topic, and takes turns describing things around it.

Hidden among them is **The Pear**, who looks and plays exactly like everyone else.

Also hidden is **The Dark Potato**, who wasn't given the real topic at all — they were given a decoy, and have to bluff their way through describing something they know nothing about.

The Dark Potato is hunting The Pear. Everyone else is hunting The Dark Potato.

---

## 2. THE ROLES

### 🐭 Jerry — *the detective*
**Always exactly 1. Publicly visible to everyone, including The Dark Potato.**

Jerry receives the round's topic. Jerry does **not** post clues — Jerry watches everyone else describe it, looking for the person whose clues don't quite fit.

Jerry cannot be voted out by the community.

Jerry carries **Ahh Shoot** — a personal elimination shot. Fired at a Dark Potato, it kills them. Fired at anyone innocent, Jerry dies instead.

### 🍐 The Pear — *the sacred one*
**Always exactly 1. Hidden.**

The Pear receives two topic-related words and posts clues, exactly like a Ramen Bowl. The Pear's only job is to not be identified.

If The Pear is eliminated by any means, The Dark Potato wins instantly.

### 🍜 Ramen Bowl — *the fakes*
**Hidden. Fills the remaining seats.**

Receives two topic-related words and posts clues. Ramen Bowls are the crowd The Pear hides inside. They win with the good team.

### 🥔 The Dark Potato — *the imposter*
**1–5 depending on player count. Hidden.**

Does **not** receive the real topic. Receives a decoy topic instead (e.g. *mouldy cheese*) and must post clues convincing enough to pass.

Goal: get The Pear eliminated. Carries seven abilities — see the reference in section 7.

**With multiple Dark Potatoes:** they do **not** know each other's identities. They have no shared chat, no shared abilities, and each has their own full ability set. They may target each other freely.

### 💩 Diarrhea — *Sylly Mode only*
**Exactly 1, only when Sylly Mode is on. Hidden.**

Plays exactly like a Ramen Bowl — two words, posts clues — and wins with the good team. Additionally carries **Get Pooped On**, which can strip abilities off a Dark Potato.

### Dealing roles

Choose your player count, toggle Sylly Mode and extra imposters if you want them, and hit **Randomize**.

Pass the tablet around using your real names. Each player taps to reveal their own secret role, hides it, and passes on. Jerry's identity is announced publicly once everyone has looked.

## 4. HOW A ROUND WORKS

**Step 1 — Ingredients are dealt.**
- 🐭 Jerry sees the topic. *(e.g. "instant noodles")*
- 🍐🍜💩 Everyone else sees two words connected to it, different for each player. *(e.g. "dry, hot" / "minutes, cheap" / "salty, packet")*
- 🥔 The Dark Potato sees a decoy topic instead. *(e.g. "mouldy cheese")*

**Step 2 — Clues.**
Every player except Jerry posts one message in the group chat that relates to their words. Jerry stays silent and reads.

During this phase, whispers are open and Dark Potato abilities are live.

**Step 3 — Voting.**
The community votes. Jerry may fire Ahh Shoot, Dark Potatoes may Murder, Diarrhea may Get Pooped On.

**Step 4 — Repeat.**
If nobody has won, a brand new topic and a fresh set of words are dealt, and the game continues from Step 1. Whispers are wiped between rounds.

---

## 5. VOTING

- Everybody still alive votes, including The Dark Potato and Jerry.
- Anyone may be voted **except Jerry**, who is immune.
- **Skip** is always available as an option.
- A player is eliminated on a **majority of living players**. If no option reaches a majority, or if there's a tie, **nobody is eliminated** and the game moves to the next round.
- Ability actions (Ahh Shoot, Murder, Get Pooped On) are locked in during this same phase and resolve after the vote is counted.

---

## 6. DEATHS & ELIMINATIONS

**Honourable Death** — a Ramen Bowl or Diarrhea is voted out. Their role is *not* revealed. Everyone's screen shows **"Honourable Death."**

**The Pear Has Been Moldified** — The Pear is eliminated by any means at all. The Dark Potato wins immediately.

**Sad Death** — Jerry fires Ahh Shoot at an innocent player. Both the target and Jerry die. Jerry's ability is gone for the rest of the game, and Jerry becomes a spectator, but the game continues.

**Spectating** — dead players see everyone's true roles, and can read every whisper in the game (shown as `name → name`). They cannot vote, speak, or use abilities.

**Immune** — abilities used on dead players never work. This is always on.

---

## 7. ABILITIES REFERENCE

### 🐭 Jerry

**Ahh Shoot** — Fire during the voting phase at one player (not yourself). Hit a Dark Potato and they die. Hit an innocent and you die alongside them. Hit The Pear and The Dark Potato wins outright — and you die too.
*Limit: one shot per voting phase. Total shots across the game equal the number of Dark Potatoes in play.*

### 🥔 The Dark Potato

**Murder** — During the voting phase, secretly select one player. They die, regardless of the vote result. Cannot target Jerry.
*Limit: once per voting phase. Resets every round.*

**Eat Ramen** — Silence one player for the entire round. They cannot post clues or whisper until voting begins. Works on anyone, not just Ramen Bowls.
*Limit: once per game.*

**Face Reveal** — See one player's true role. To unlock it, you must work an assigned word from the food bank into your normal clue message that round — slip it in too obviously and you'll draw suspicion.
*Limit: once per game.*

**Stalking** — Read one chosen player's private whispers for the rest of the round.
*Limit: once per voting phase. Resets every round.*

**Swapsies** — Change what one player sees their own role as. A Ramen Bowl might look down and see "The Pear." Nothing actually changes — it's pure confusion.
*Limit: once per voting phase. Resets every round.*

**Impersonating** — Whisper to any player, including Jerry, while appearing to be someone else entirely.
*Limit: once per round.*

**Dark Protection** — 🤪 *Sylly Mode only.* If Jerry fires Ahh Shoot at you, it's deflected: you survive, a random Ramen Bowl dies in your place, and Jerry dies too. Only works when there are multiple Ramen Bowls still alive. Armed in advance during the round, not automatic.
*Limit: once per game.*

### 💩 Diarrhea

**Get Pooped On** — During the voting phase, secretly pick one player. If they are a Dark Potato, one of their abilities is chosen at random and disabled permanently. If you're wrong, nothing happens. **You are never told which.** The target sees a popup reading *"You got pooped on."*
*Limit: once per voting phase. Resets every round.*

### 🌐 Everyone

**Whisper** — Send private messages to any living player. All whispers are wiped at the end of each round. Dead players can read everything.

---

## 8. WINNING

### 🥔 The Dark Potato wins if:
- **The Pear is eliminated** — by community vote, by Murder, or by Jerry's Ahh Shoot. Any of them.
- **Only one living player remains who isn't a Dark Potato.**

### 🐭🍐🍜💩 Jerry, The Pear, the Ramen Bowls and Diarrhea win if:
- **Every Dark Potato has been eliminated** — by community vote or by Ahh Shoot.

Everyone on this side wins together, as one team. Jerry still counts as a winner even if Jerry died earlier in the game.

**When the game ends, every player's true role is revealed.**

## 9. OPEN QUESTIONS

These four are written above exactly as you described them, but each one has a consequence worth a second look.

### ⚠️ 1. The Pear has no tell — so The Dark Potato is guessing blind

The Pear receives two topic words and posts clues, identically to a Ramen Bowl. There is nothing about The Pear that a Dark Potato could ever notice, because there is nothing different to notice.

That makes finding The Pear a coin flip rather than a deduction. In a 5-player game the Dark Potato picks between three indistinguishable people and has a 1-in-3 chance of winning on the spot, with skill playing almost no part.

*Possible directions:* give The Pear a subtly different prompt (one off-topic word, or the topic itself instead of related words) so a sharp Dark Potato can spot the seam; or give The Pear a small forced tell like always posting first; or make Face Reveal the intended way to find them.

### ⚠️ 2. Murder makes The Dark Potato very strong at low counts

Murder is an unconditional kill, once per round, and killing The Pear wins the game. Combined with the point above, a 5-player Dark Potato can simply murder someone at random each round with roughly a 1-in-3 chance of instantly winning — without bluffing, deducing, or convincing anyone of anything. The clue-giving and voting become decoration.

*Possible directions:* make Murder a hidden second vote that stacks on the tally rather than a kill; limit it to once per game; or require the Dark Potato to have used Face Reveal first.

### ⚠️ 3. "Catch them all" can drag at high player counts

The good team wins only when every Dark Potato is dead. At 12 players with five independent Dark Potatoes — each with a Murder every round — the good team needs five successful majority votes while losing at least one player per round to Murder alone. It's worth sanity-checking whether that game can actually be finished.

### ⚠️ 4. Swapsies may not do anything

Swapsies changes what one player sees their own role as. But a player already knows what they were dealt — a Ramen Bowl who suddenly reads "The Pear" will assume a bug or an ability, not believe it. And since roles are secret anyway, that player has no way to act differently on the false information.

*Possible direction:* have Swapsies alter what one player sees *about someone else* — for instance, showing them a false Jerry — which would give it real bite.
