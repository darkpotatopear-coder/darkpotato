The Dark Potato — Questions for the Creator
Before this can be built, a few core mechanics need to be pinned down. Grouped by topic below — no pressure to answer all at once, but the first section is the most important.

1. The core win action (most important)
The Dark Potato's main win condition is "correctly typing in the name of The Pear," but the Step 1–4 round structure (get ingredients → give clues → vote → repeat) never actually includes this action.

When does the Dark Potato make this guess? Is it something they can do at any point during a round, only after the community vote, or only after being caught?

Can they guess more than once, or is it a single locked-in attempt for the whole game?

If they guess wrong (name a Ramen Bowl instead of the Pear), does that end the game immediately in the Pear/Ramen team's favor, or does play continue? (The rules currently say both — "the shield worked" implies an instant win, but the next line says the game continues until a vote succeeds.)

2. What happens on a successful community vote
If the whole-server/majority community vote correctly lands on The Dark Potato, does that immediately win the game for the Pear/Ramen side — same as Jerry's "ahh shoot" ability?

If so, what does Jerry's ability actually add, given the risk that a wrong "ahh shoot" kills Jerry too?

3. Diarrhea's ability ("Get pooped on")
The role summary says Diarrhea "discards an ability from the Dark Potato," but the detailed ability text describes a secret vote on a person, not a vote on an ability.

Does Diarrhea vote for a suspected Dark Potato, and if correct, one of that Dark Potato's abilities gets disabled? If so, chosen by whom — Diarrhea or automatically?

Or does Diarrhea instead pick directly from a list of the Dark Potato's abilities?

4. Sylly Mode player count
The Silly Mode button description says it's only available at 5+ players, but a separate note says turning it on requires a minimum of 4 players. Which is correct?

At 4 players (Jerry, Pear, Dark Potato, 1 Ramen Bowl), there's no open seat for Diarrhea — should Diarrhea replace the Ramen Bowl at that tier, or should 4 players simply not be allowed with Silly Mode on?

5. Diarrhea's win condition text
The current text for "Diarrhea Wins" repeats Jerry's win condition verbatim and doesn't actually describe a Diarrhea-specific trigger. What should Diarrhea's own win condition say?

6. Multiple Dark Potatoes (6+ players)
With up to 5 Dark Potatoes possible at higher player counts:

Do all Dark Potatoes need to agree on who to guess as the Pear, or does any single correct guess by one of them win for the whole team?

Are abilities (murder, Eat ramen, Dark protection, Face reveal, Stalking, Swapsies, impersonating) tracked per individual Dark Potato, or shared as one pool across the team?

7. "Face reveal" ability
This ability requires the Dark Potato to "complete a task" to unlock it — what is the task? Is it the same for every game, random, or set by the group?

8. Wrongful ejections
Voting out a Ramen Bowl, Pear, or Diarrhea currently does nothing and reveals nothing ("honourable death"). Is that intentional — i.e. is there meant to be zero risk to wrongly voting out a non-Jerry player — or should there be some cost to keep voting tense?

9. Ability overload check
The Dark Potato currently has seven named abilities plus the core Pear-guessing objective. Worth asking directly: should all seven ship in the first version, or would it help to launch with a smaller set and add the rest later once the core loop is tested?
