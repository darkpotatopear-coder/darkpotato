    How to Play 🥔

    The Dark Potato hunts The Pear. Everyone else hunts The Dark Potato.

    The Roles

    • 🐭 Jerry — the detective. Everyone knows who Jerry is. Sees the secret food, gives no clue, can't be voted out.
    • 🍐 The Pear — the sacred one. Gets two words, and they're always the food's colour and taste. Don't get found.
    • 🍜 Ramen Bowls — the fakes. Two other words about the food. Act like The Pear so The Dark Potato goes after you instead.
    • 🥔 The Dark Potato — the imposter. No food, just mouldy cheese. Bluff, and find The Pear. Several Dark Potatoes don't know each other.
    • 💩 Diarrhea — Sylly Mode only. Plays like a Ramen Bowl, can't be murdered, and can poop on a suspect.

    Step 1

    Get your ingredients

    Pass the phone round. Each player sees their card in secret, can send one Whisper, and Dark Potatoes can use their tricks.

    Step 2

    Clue time

    Phone in the middle. In the order shown, everyone except Jerry says one clue out loud about their words — without saying the words. Then argue about who's sus.

    Step 3

    Vote

    Pass the phone round again. Read your whispers and vote in secret — anyone except Jerry, or Skip. Someone goes out only if more than half of the living players vote for them.

    Results go in this order: Jerry's shot → the vote → Diarrhea's poop → the Dark Potato's murder. Get knocked out first and your own move fizzles.

    Step 4

    Repeat

    Nobody won? New food, new words, whispers wiped. Go again.

    Abilities

    • 🐭 Ahh Shoot — on the vote, Jerry can shoot instead. The community vote is skipped. Hit a Dark Potato: they die. Hit anyone else: they die and so does Jerry. One shot per Dark Potato in the game.
    • 🥔 Murder — every vote, pick someone to take out (not Jerry). Several Dark Potatoes pool their picks; most picks wins.
    • 🥔 Eat Ramen — silence someone for the round: no clue, no whisper. Once a game.
    • 🥔 Face Reveal — slip your secret food-bank word into your round-1 clue, then see one player's real role. Once a game.
    • 🥔 Stalking — read every whisper to and from one player this round.
    • 🥔 Swapsies — next round, someone's card shows a fake role and fake words. Nothing really changes.
    • 🥔 Impersonating — send a whisper that looks like it came from someone else. Even Jerry.
    • 🥔 Dark Protection — Sylly Mode. Arm it; if Jerry shoots you that round and 2+ Ramen Bowls are alive, a random Ramen Bowl dies instead — and Jerry too. Once a game.
    • 💩 Get Pooped On — on the vote, pick a suspect. If it's a Dark Potato, one of their abilities is gone for good. You're never told if you got it.

    Deaths

    • Honourable Death — a Ramen Bowl or Diarrhea goes out. Their role stays a mystery.
    • Sad Death — Jerry shot an innocent. They both go.
    • Spectating — ghosts can open the Ghost Corner at clue time to see every role and whisper. Ghosts don't talk.
    • Immune — you can't use abilities on ghosts.

    Winning

    • 🥔 Dark Potatoes win if The Pear dies — voted out (The Pear Has Been Moldified), murdered, or shot by Jerry (The Pear Fell Into The Hands Of The Spammer) — or if only one living player isn't a Dark Potato.
    • 🐭🍐🍜💩 Everyone else wins together once every Dark Potato is gone. Ghosts on the team win too.
