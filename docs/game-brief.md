# Game Brief — Da most aura

> Stage 1. Status: **CONFIRMED** (2026-09-29). The owner handed the design over completely ("it is
> your project"), so this brief is Claude's reading of the creator's notes in `notes/`, weighted
> towards the creator's original notes and their follow-up answers where the sources disagree.
> Section 12 lists every place the notes were interpreted, so the creator can see what changed.

## 1. The pitch
Jerry knows the secret food. Everyone else gets two clue words about it — except The Dark Potato,
who got mouldy cheese and has to bluff. The Dark Potato is hunting The Pear; everyone else is
hunting The Dark Potato.

## 2. Players and table
- Player count: minimum **4** (5 with Sylly Mode), maximum **12**, best at **6–9**.
- Length of a game: 10–25 minutes (usually 2–4 rounds).
- One phone, passed around. It sits in the middle of the table (public) for: the Jerry
  announcement, clue time, and the results of every vote.

## 3. Theme and voice
A cursed kitchen. Food, betrayal, and a lot of aura. The voice is a group chat of 13-year-olds —
cheeky, a bit gross, never mean.
- "You got mouldy cheese. No topic for you — listen hard and blend in."
- "Gus got their ramen eaten. Silent until the vote."
- "The Pear Has Been Moldified."

Colour: deep aura purple. Emoji: 🥔.

## 4. How a game goes
1. **Setup** (host, public). Player count, names, Sylly Mode, how many Dark Potatoes.
2. **Jerry is announced** (public). Everyone learns who Jerry is.
3. **Deal** (each living player in turn, PRIVATE, pass gate before each). See your role and your
   ingredients; write one whisper; Dark Potatoes use their before-clue abilities.
4. **Clue time** (phone in the middle, PUBLIC). The screen gives a speaking order. Each player
   except Jerry says one clue out loud about their words, without saying the words. Then open
   discussion. An optional timer runs.
5. **Vote** (each living player in turn, PRIVATE, pass gate before each). Read your whispers, cast
   your community vote. Jerry may fire Ahh Shoot. Dark Potatoes pick a Murder. Diarrhea may poop.
6. **Results** (PUBLIC). The shot, the vote tally, the murder — one beat at a time.
7. If nobody has won, go to 3 with a new food. Otherwise **game over** (PUBLIC): the winner and
   every player's true role.

## 5. Rules
**Roles** (counts from the creator's table; Dark Potatoes default to 1, the host can add more up to
the cap):

| Players | 🐭 Jerry | 🍐 Pear | 🥔 Dark Potato (cap) | 🍜 Ramen Bowls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 5 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 6 | 1 | 1 | 2 | the rest |
| 7 | 1 | 1 | 2 | the rest |
| 8 | 1 | 1 | 3 | the rest |
| 9 | 1 | 1 | 3 | the rest |
| 10 | 1 | 1 | 4 | the rest |
| 11 | 1 | 1 | 4 | the rest |
| 12 | 1 | 1 | 5 | the rest |

Sylly Mode (5+): adds 1 💩 Diarrhea to the player count and uses the cap from the row above.

- **🐭 Jerry** — public. Sees the food. Gives no clue. Can't be voted out or murdered.
- **🍐 The Pear** — hidden. Their two words are always the food's **colour** and **taste**.
- **🍜 Ramen Bowl** — hidden. Two other words about the food. Job: act like The Pear so the Dark
  Potato murders them instead.
- **🥔 The Dark Potato** — hidden. Gets no food ("You got mouldy cheese"). Several Dark Potatoes
  don't know each other.
- **💩 Diarrhea** — Sylly Mode only, hidden. Two words like a Ramen Bowl. Can't be murdered.

**A round:** Deal → Clue time → Vote → Results.

**Community vote:** every living player votes, secretly, on the phone. Any living player except
Jerry and yourself, or Skip. A player goes out only with votes from **more than half of the living
players**; otherwise nobody goes out.

**Order of results:** Jerry's shot (if fired, the community vote is skipped) → community vote →
Get Pooped On → Murder. Anyone knocked out by the shot or vote loses their own secret action that
round.

**Abilities**
| Who | Ability | When | Limit | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Everyone | Whisper | Deal | 1 per round | A private note to one living player; they read it on their vote turn. Wiped each round. |
| 🐭 Jerry | Ahh Shoot | Vote | 1 per round; total = number of Dark Potatoes | Name a player instead of the community vote. Dark Potato: they die. Anyone else: they die **and Jerry dies** (Sad Death). The Pear: Dark Potatoes win. |
| 🥔 | Murder | Vote | Every round | Secret pick. With several Dark Potatoes, their picks are pooled: most picks dies, ties drawn at random. Not Jerry. |
| 🥔 | Eat Ramen | Deal | Once per game | Silence a player this round: no clue, no whisper. Shown to the table. |
| 🥔 | Face Reveal | Deal, from round 2 | Once per game | Unlocked by slipping a secret food-bank word into your round-1 clue (honour system). See one player's true role. |
| 🥔 | Stalking | Deal | Every round | Read every whisper to or from one player this round (on your vote turn). |
| 🥔 | Swapsies | Deal | Every round | Next round, one player's card shows a fake role and fake words. Nothing really changes. |
| 🥔 | Impersonating | Deal | Every round | Send a whisper that looks like it came from someone else (even Jerry). |
| 🥔 | Dark Protection | Deal (arm) | Once per game, Sylly only | If Jerry shoots you this round while 2+ Ramen Bowls live: you survive, a random Ramen Bowl dies, Jerry dies. |
| 💩 | Get Pooped On | Vote | Every round | Secret pick. If it's a Dark Potato, one of their abilities is disabled for good. Diarrhea is never told. The target sees "You got pooped on". |

**Immune:** abilities can't target dead players. **Spectating:** dead players (ghosts) may open the
Ghost Corner at clue time to see every role and every whisper. Ghosts don't speak or vote.

**Winning**
- 🥔 **Dark Potatoes win** if The Pear dies (any way), or if only one living player is not a Dark
  Potato.
- 🐭🍐🍜💩 **Everyone else wins together** when every Dark Potato is dead. Dead teammates still win.

## 6. Terminology
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ingredients | What your card shows: the food (Jerry), two words, or mouldy cheese |
| Honourable Death | A Ramen Bowl or Diarrhea is voted out or murdered. Role stays hidden. |

| The Pear Has Been Moldified | The Pear is voted out or murdered. Dark Potatoes win. |
| The Pear Fell Into The Hands Of The Spammer | Jerry shoots The Pear. Dark Potatoes win. |
| Ghost / Spectating | A dead player; sees all roles and whispers. |
| Sylly Mode | Adds Diarrhea and Dark Protection. |

## 8. What we're borrowing
- **Among Us** — one imposter team, everyone else together; majority vote with Skip; ghosts.
- **Spyfall / Insider / Undercover** — one player with no topic bluffing through a clue round.
- **Avalon's Merlin / Assassin** — The Pear must hide from the Dark Potato's single guess, and
  Ramen Bowls act as decoys.
- **Werewolf (pass-the-phone apps)** — every living player gets a private turn each phase, so
  nobody can tell who has powers by how long they hold the phone.

## 10. Decided against
- **Typed chat** — one phone; clues are spoken, only whispers are typed.
- **Dark Potato gets a similar decoy food** — the creator's "You got mouldy cheese" is funnier and
  makes the bluff the real skill.
- **Murder resolving before the vote** — a caught Dark Potato could still win by a blind guess;
  the table's read should come first.
- **Face Reveal usable in round 1** — with Murder it wins round one with no bluffing.
- **Swapsies changing your own role label only** — no bite; fake words make it a framing tool.
- **Diarrhea at 4 players** — creator: Sylly Mode adds a seat, it doesn't replace one.

## 11. Open questions
None blocking. Watch in play: whether Murder every round is too strong at 4–5 players, and whether
a majority of living players is too hard to reach at 11–12.
